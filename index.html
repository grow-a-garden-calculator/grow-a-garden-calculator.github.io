UNDER THE HOOD

How the value is worked out

Value = Base Value × Weight Multiplier × Mutation Multiplier × Quantity

Base value

Fixed per crop, tied to its rarity. This never changes.

Weight multiplier

Heavier plants are worth more. Weight scales the base value up from the crop's average.

Mutation multiplier

Every selected mutation's multiplier is added together, then applied on top of the base and weight value.

Quantity

Simple multiplier if you're valuing more than one of the same plant at once.

QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions

How is a plant's value calculated in Grow a Garden?

Value is worked out from the plant's base Sheckle value, multiplied by a weight factor based on how heavy it grew, then multiplied again by the combined strength of any mutations stacked on it, and finally by the quantity you're selling.

Can mutations stack?

Yes. Selecting more than one mutation adds their multipliers together before they're applied to the plant's base value, so a Gold and Rainbow plant is worth far more than either mutation alone.

Does weight matter that much?

It's one of the biggest factors. A plant grown well past its average weight can be worth several times more than a plant of the same type and mutations at a lower weight.

Is this calculator free?

Yes — it's completely free to use, with no account or sign-up required.

Are the values exact?

Base values and mutation multipliers are community-sourced estimates for planning trades, not official numbers pulled from the game's files. Always confirm the in-game sell price before a big trade.
